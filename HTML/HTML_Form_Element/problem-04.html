Leave Categories and Descriptions

Leave Category Description
Casual Leave For personal or short-term requirements.
Sick Leave For illness, medical appointments or health-related needs.
Earned Leave Leave accumulated and used for planned vacations or personal work.
Work From Home Allows employees to work remotely instead of taking complete leave.
Other For leave requirements that do not fit the above categories.
