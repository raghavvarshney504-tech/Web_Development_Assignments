Introduction: E-Commerce Checkout and Delivery

E-Commerce Checkout and Delivery is an important part of online shopping that helps customers complete their purchases and receive products at their doorstep. The checkout process allows users to review their selected products, enter delivery details, choose a payment method, and confirm their order. After the order is placed, the delivery process ensures that the products are packed, shipped, tracked, and delivered safely to the customer . A simple and secure checkout system combined with reliable delivery provides a smooth shopping experience and increases customer satisfaction.
