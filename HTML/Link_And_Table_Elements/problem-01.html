(Admission Overview):

@GLA Universityoffers undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma, and doctoral programmes in areas such as Engineering, Computer Science, Management, Pharmacy, Law, Biotechnology, Commerce, and Basic Sciences. For the 2026–27 session, admissions are open for various programmes.

(Eligibility)
GLA University offers admission to a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes, and the eligibility criteria vary according to the course selected by the student. Generally, candidates must have completed the required qualifying examination from a recognized board, university, or institution and must have studied the subjects prescribed for their chose programme. For undergraduate programmes, students are normally required to pass the 10+2 examination with the relevant subjects and minimum percentage specified by the university. For example, applicants seeking admission B.Tech programmes must have passed 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics compulsory subjects, along with Chemistry or Computer Science. For B.Tech programmes at GLA University Greater Noida, candidates are required to have at least 50% marks in the specified subjects taken together and 50% marks overall.

(Important Terms):

Eligibility Criteria
Candidates seeking admission to GLA University must fulfill the eligibility requirements of their chosen course.For undergraduated programs, candidates generally need to have passed Class 12th from a recognized board with the required subjects and minimum marks.
Merit List
GLA University may prepare a merit list based on the candidate's academic performance and applicable entrance examination scores. Candidates included in the merit list may proceed to the next stage of the admission process.
Counselling
Shortlisted candidates participate in the counselling process. During counselling, students receive guidance regarding available courses, seats, and admission requirements and complete the required admission formalities.
Scholarship
GLA University offers scholarships to eligible students based on criteria such as academic performance, entrance examination performance, and other applicable achievements. Scholarship conditions may vary according to the program and admission year.
Registration
Candidates can register for admission by filling out the university's application form and providing the required personal, academic, and contact information. Applicants should submit the necessary documents and complete the applicable admission formalities.
