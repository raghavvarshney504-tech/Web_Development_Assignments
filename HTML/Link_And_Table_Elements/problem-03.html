Computer Science And Engineering


(Introduction):
Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) is a field of engineering that combines computer science principles with engineering techniques to develop software, computer systems, and innovative technologies. It covers important areas such as programming , data structures, algorithms, databases, computer networks, operating systems, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and cybersecurity. CSE helps students develop problem-solving, logical thinking, and technical skills required to create efficient and innovative solutions. With the rapid growth of digital technology, CSE offers opportunities in areas such as software development, web development, data s cience, AI/ML, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and research.

Lecture
A lecture is a classroom teaching session where the teacher explains concepts, theories, formulas, and important topics related to a subject.
Lab
A laboratory session allows students to apply theoretical knowledge through experiments, programming exercises, measurements, and practical activities.
Tutorial
A tutorial is a smaller academic session focused on solving numerical problems, clearing doubts, practicing concepts, and improving understanding .
Internal Assessment
Internal assessment is the evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, quizzes, presentations, attendance, and other academic activities.
Practical
A practical is an activity in which students perform experiments or hands-on tasks to understand and apply the concepts learned in lectures.
