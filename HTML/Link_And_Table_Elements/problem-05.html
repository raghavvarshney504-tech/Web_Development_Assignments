Annual College Fest 2026

Welcome to our three-day celebration of talent, technology and creativity!

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day college event designed to provide students with opportunities to showcase their talent, creativity, technical skills and teamwork.

The fest includes cultural activities, technical competitions, individual events, team events, workshops and exciting final rounds. Students from different departments can participate and compete.

Event Definitions

Open Event
An event in which students from different departments or colleges can participate.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants work together as a team.
Individual Event
An event in which a participant competes individually.
Registration
The process of submitting participant details to enter a fest event.
Coordinator
A person responsible for managing an event and assisting participants.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition where selected participants compete for the winner position.

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest registration page.
  2. Choose the event in which you want to participate.
  3. Fill in your name, department, email and contact details.
  4. Select individual or team participation.
  5. Pay the applicable registration fee.
  6. Submit the registration form.
  7. Save your registration/reference number.
  8. Report to the venue before the scheduled event.

General Fest Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue before the event starts.
  • Registration must be completed before the deadline.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of event coordinators.
  • Misconduct or unfair practices may lead to disqualification.
  • Participants must maintain cleanliness on campus.
  • Use of unauthorized material or equipment is not allowed.
  • Decisions of the judging panel will be final.
