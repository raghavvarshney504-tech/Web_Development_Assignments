About the Bank

A bank is a financial institution that provides a wide range of services to individuals, businesses, and organizations. It accepts deposits, provides loans, enables money transfers, and offers services such as savings accounts, online banking, debit cards, and investment facilities. Banks play an important role in the economy by helping people manage their money and supporting businesses through financial services. With modern digital banking, customers can access many banking services quickly and securely from anywhere.
