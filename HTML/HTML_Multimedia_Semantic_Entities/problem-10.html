Welcome to National Tech & Career Expo

The National Tech & Career Expo 2026 is a large-scale technology and career event designed for students, graduates, developers, entrepreneurs and professionals. The expo provides opportunities to explore companies, internships, placements, workshops, certifications and networking opportunities.

Learn today, connect tomorrow and build your career for the future.

"The future belongs to those who prepare for it today."

About the Expo

Event Introduction

National Tech & Career Expo brings together leading technology companies, educational institutions, recruiters, industry experts and students under one platform.

Attendees can discover career opportunities, attend technical sessions, participate in workshops, meet industry professionals and learn about the latest developments in technology.

Organizer Message

Welcome to the National Tech & Career Expo. Our goal is to connect talented students with organizations and experts who can help them build successful careers.

"Connect with industry, improve your skills and take the next step towards your professional journey."

Dr. Arjun Sharma
Expo Director

Important Information

  • Carry a valid student or professional ID.
  • Bring multiple copies of your resume.
  • Register for workshops in advance.
  • Maintain professional communication.
  • Reach the venue at least 30 minutes early.

Attendee Journey

  1. Register for the Expo
  2. Receive your confirmation
  3. Check in at the venue
  4. Explore participating companies
  5. Attend workshops and speaker sessions
  6. Interact with recruiters
  7. Apply for suitable opportunities
  8. Build your professional network

Preparation Tips

  • Prepare an updated resume.
  • Research participating companies.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Practice common interview questions.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Carry your laptop if required for workshops.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your registration confirmation available.
