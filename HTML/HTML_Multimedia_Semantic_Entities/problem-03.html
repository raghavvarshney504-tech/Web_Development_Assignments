Destination Overview

Agra is one of the most famous tourist destinations in India. It is located on the banks of the Yamuna River and is famous for its historical monuments and Mughal architecture.

The city is home to the world-famous Taj Mahal and offers visitors a combination of history, culture, food and shopping.
