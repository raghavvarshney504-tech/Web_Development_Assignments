Students Terms And Rules:


Students are expected to follow the rules and regulations of the institution, maintain discipline and attend classes regularly, and respect teachers and fellow students. These terms help create a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for everyone.


(General Rules):

(i) Students must attend classes regularly and be punctual.
(ii) Students must carry their identity card on campus.
(iii) Students should maintain discipline and respect teachers, staff, and fellow students.
(iv) Students must keep the campus clean and use facilities responsibly.
(v) Students should submit assignments, projects, and other work on time.
(vi) Students must follow the academic and examination rules of the institution.

(Attendance):

(i) Students must attend classes regularly and maintain the required attendance percentage.
(ii) Students should arrive in class before the scheduled time.
(iii)< Students must mark their attendance honestly and correctly.
(iv) Prior permission should be taken from the concerned authority for planned absences.
(v) Students with low attendance may be subject to academic restrictions as per university rules.
(vi) Medical or other approved leave must be supported by valid documents when required.


(Examination):


(i) Students must reach the examination hall before the scheduled time.
(ii) Students must bring only the permitted stationery and materials into the examination hall.
(iii) Students must follow all instructions given by the invigilator.
(iv) Students must maintain silence and avoid disturbing other students.
(v) Students must not communicate or share answers with other students. (vi) Students must submit their answer sheets before leaving the examination hall.
