Chemistry

Chemistry deals with the composition, structure, properties, and reactions of matter.

Chemical Formulas

1. Water: H2O

2. Carbon Dioxide: CO2

3. Oxygen: O2

4. Sulfuric Acid: H2SO4

5. Ammonia: NH3

6. Methane: CH4

Important: Atoms combine to form molecules and compounds.

Physics

Physics is the branch of science that studies matter, energy, motion, force, and their interactions.

Scientific Expressions

1. Area of a square = a2

2. Volume of a cube = a3

3. Speed = Distance / Time

4. Einstein's equation: E = mc2

5. Acceleration = m/s2

Force is an interaction that can change the motion of an object.

Newton's Second Law: F = ma

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms, including plants, animals, and microorganisms.

Important Biology Terms

Cell A cell is the fundamental structural, functional, and biological unit of all living organisms. Often referred to as the "building block of life," it is the smallest unit that can replicate independently. Cells have many parts, each with a different function. Some of these parts, called organelles, are specialized structures that perform certain tasks \ within the cell. Human cells contain the following major parts

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms. DNA is the set of instructions that make you, you. T hese instructions tell your body how to create organs and tissues, how to function and what you look like. You can think of DNA as a vast library of instruction manuals. It uses its own “language” to write the story of you that your cells can read. All living things have DNA in their cells (and some viruses carry it with them, too). You inherit your DNA from your biological parents — you get half from your mother and half from your father.

Photosynthesis: Plants use sunlight, carbon dioxide (CO2), and water (H2O) to produce food and oxygen (O2).
