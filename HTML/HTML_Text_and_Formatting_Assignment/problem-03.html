"India will end physical boarding pass stamping for international flyers starting September 1."


Starting September 1, 2026, international passengers departing from Indian airports will no longer need physical boarding pass stamping at immigration counters. Travelers can show digital e-boarding passes on smartphones, while paper passes remain optional.


"E-boarding passes accepted for immigration clearance"

(i) Starting September 1, India’s Bureau of Immigration will end mandatory physical boarding pass stamping for international departures at all international airports, allowing passengers to present digital e-boarding passes on smartphones for clearance while continuing to accept optional printed passes without stamps.

(ii)The Bureau of Immigration under the Ministry of Home Affairs has issued a formal directive to all major airport operators and security stakeholders, including the Central Industrial Security Force, declaring that the long-standing practice of stamping physical boarding passes at immigration checkpoints will cease. According to administrative reviews, the old manual requirement created unwanted bottlenecks and passenger congestion during peak departure hours. Furthermore, authorities noted frequent functional errors where smudged, improper, or incorrect ink stamps interfered with automated barcode readers and manual document inspections

(iii)Airports are increasingly adopting digital technology to make international travel faster and more convenient. The acceptance of e-boarding passes for immigration clearance allows passengers to use a boarding pass displayed on their smartphone or other electronic device instead of carrying a printed copy. This reduces paperwork, saves time, and provides a smoother experience at the airport. Passengers should ensure that their digital boarding pass is easily accessible and that their phone has sufficient battery before reaching immigration.
