About The College:

GLA University is a well-known private university in Uttar Pradesh, with campuses in Mathura and Greater Noida.GLA University aims to provide students with a combination of academic knowledge, practical skills, innovation, industry exposure, and personal development, Its Greater Noida campus emphasizes industry-integrated learning, practical projects, mentorship, and exposure to real-world applications. GLA University states that it has NAAC A+ accreditation and a large alumni network.

Courses Offered by GLA University

The university provides courses in Engineering and Technology, Management, Pharmacy, Biotechnology, Law, Commerce, Education, and Science .Students can choose programs such as B.Tech, BCA, BBA, B.Pharm, MBA, MCA, M.Tech, M.Pharm, and Ph.D. Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

The university also organizes fests, competitions, workshops, and other events throughout the year Sports are an important part of campus life, with facilities and activities for games such as cricket, football, basketball, volleyball, badminton, athletics, chess, and table tennis. Students can also participate in music, dance, theatre, art, coding, robotics, cybersecurity, and other clubs.
