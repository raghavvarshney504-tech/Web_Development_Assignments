Event Overview

About the National Career & Education Fair

The National Career & Education Fair 2026 is a two-day career and education event designed to help students, graduates and job seekers discover opportunities in employment, higher education, internships, placements, scholarships and apprenticeships.

The event will bring together leading organizations, educational institutions, career experts and students at a common platform. Participants will have the opportunity to communicate with representatives, attend expert sessions, explore career paths and understand the skills required by modern industries.

The fair focuses on connecting education with employment. It will provide guidance for participants who are planning their first career, changing their career direction or looking for further education.

Event Details

  • Date: 17 - 18 October 2026
  • Venue: India Expo Centre, Greater Noida
  • Expected Participants: Students and Job Seekers
  • Event Type: Career and Education Fair
  • Entry: Registration Required

Objectives of the Event

  1. Connect students with potential employers.
  2. Provide information about higher education opportunities.
  3. Promote internships and apprenticeship programs.
  4. Help participants understand current industry requirements.
  5. Provide career counselling and professional guidance.
  6. Introduce participants to scholarship opportunities.
  7. Encourage networking between students and professionals.

Participant Guidance

Participants are advised to research organizations before attending the fair. Carry an updated resume and prepare a short introduction about your education, skills and career interests.

Participants should attend sessions according to their interests and communicate professionally with organization representatives.

"Our goal is to create a bridge between education, skills and employment and help every participant make an informed career decision."
— National Career & Education Fair Organizing Committee

Preparation Guide

How to Prepare for the Fair

Proper preparation can help participants make the most of the career fair. Research the organizations, identify your preferred career sectors and prepare questions before arriving at the venue.

Important Preparation Tips

  • Carry multiple copies of your updated resume.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare a short self-introduction.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Prepare questions about jobs and internships.
  • Keep important academic information ready.
  • Arrive before your selected sessions begin.
  • Maintain professional communication with recruiters.

Notice: Session timings may be changed by the organizers.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete the online registration.
  2. Receive the registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue.
  4. Complete event check-in.
  5. Attend the orientation session.
  6. Visit organization booths.
  7. Attend career and education sessions.
  8. Interact with recruiters and counselors.
  9. Collect relevant information.
  10. Follow up with organizations after the event.

Information / FAQs

Career Fair
A Career Fair is an event where employers, educational institutions and job seekers meet to explore career, employment and education opportunities.
Internship
An internship is a temporary learning opportunity that provides practical experience in a professional environment.
Placement
Placement is the process of connecting eligible candidates with suitable employment opportunities.
Scholarship
A scholarship is financial assistance provided to eligible students to support their education.
Apprenticeship
An apprenticeship combines practical workplace training with structured learning to develop job-related skills.
Who can attend the fair?
Students, graduates and job seekers interested in career or education opportunities can register for the event.
Is registration compulsory?
Yes. Participants should complete the registration form before attending the event.
What should participants bring?
Participants should bring their registration confirmation, resume and relevant educational or professional information.
