Our Services:

Emergency rooms (ER): Immediate, round-the-clock treatment for life-threatening injuries or severe sudden illnesses.

Healthcare services include all the medical, preventive, and supportive care delivered by professionals to maintain or restore well-being.

  • General visits: Routine check-ups, physical exams, and treatment for common illnesses with a family doctor or general practitioner.
  • Preventive care: Vaccinations, health screenings (like blood pressure or diabetes checks), and lifestyle advice.
  • Specialized care: Visits to medical specialists like cardiologists, dermatologists, or pediatricians for specific conditions.
  • Tertiary services: Highly complex or specialized medical procedures handled in advanced regional or urban medical centers
  • Urgent care centers: Walk-in clinics for pressing medical issues that are not severe enough for an emergency room.

Rehabilitative Care: Physical, occupational, or speech therapy to help a person recover and rebuild skills after an injury.

Consultation

What is Consultation?
Consultation is a professional interaction between a patient and a healthcare provider. During the consultation, the patient can discuss their health concerns, symptoms, medical history, and other relevant information with the doctor.
Purpose of Consultation
The main purpose of a consultation is to understand the patient's concerns, evaluate their condition, provide appropriate medical advice, and suggest further steps such as tests, treatment, or follow-up visits when necessary.
Before Consultation
Patients should keep their previous medical reports, prescriptions, medication details, and other relevant health information ready. Arriving on time can also help make the consultation more organized.
During Consultation
The doctor may ask questions about symptoms, medical history, lifestyle, and current medications. The patient can also ask questions and discuss any concerns they have with the healthcare provider.
After Consultation
After the consultation, the doctor may provide medical advice, prescriptions, recommended tests, or instructions for a follow-up appointment. Patients should follow the healthcare provider's advice and ask for clarification if they do not understand any instruction.
Online Consultation
Online consultation allows patients to communicate with a healthcare provider through a digital platform. It can be useful for discussing general concerns and follow-up care, depending on the situation.
