Open Positions

Below are some of our current opportunities. Each position represents an independent role with its own responsibilities and requirements.

Software Engineer

Design, develop and maintain scalable software applications. Work closely with engineers and product teams to deliver reliable solutions.

Skills: Python, Java, Git and REST APIs

Data Scientist

Analyze complex datasets and develop machine-learning models that help the company make better data-driven decisions.

Skills: Python, SQL, Statistics and Machine Learning

Frontend Developer

Build responsive and accessible web interfaces using modern frontend technologies and collaborate with UI/UX designers.

Skills: HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React

Cloud Engineer

Build and maintain secure cloud infrastructure and help development teams deploy scalable applications.

Skills: Azure, AWS, Docker and Kubernetes

UI/UX Designer

Create intuitive user experiences by researching user needs, designing interfaces and collaborating with product teams.

Skills: Figma, Prototyping and User Research

Cybersecurity Analyst

Monitor security systems, investigate potential threats and help improve the security of company applications and networks.

Skills: Network Security, Linux and SIEM

Product Manager

Define product strategies, coordinate cross-functional teams and ensure that products solve important customer problems.

Skills: Product Strategy, Communication and Analytics

Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to understand your skills, experience and ability to work collaboratively.

  1. Application Submission
  2. Application Screening
  3. Technical Round
  4. HR Round
  5. Offer and Joining

Hiring Stage Details

Screening
Recruiters review your application, experience, skills and qualifications against the requirements of the position.
Technical Round
Candidates may be asked technical questions or practical problems related to the selected role.
HR Round
This discussion focuses on communication, career goals, teamwork and general workplace expectations.
Offer
Selected candidates receive an offer containing information about the role, compensation and joining process.

Recruiter Tips

Tip: Read the job description carefully before applying and highlight the skills that are relevant to the position.

Keep your resume clear and updated. Include projects, technical skills and achievements that demonstrate your abilities.
